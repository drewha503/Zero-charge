Settings

Station data comes from the U.S. Department of Energy's Alternative Fuels Data Center (covers the US and Canada). The shared DEMO_KEY allows a few dozen lookups per hour. If you see a rate-limit error, get a free key in about a minute at developer.nrel.gov/signup and paste it here. The key is stored only on this phone.

What counts as free

Each station's operator reports a pricing note. This app shows a station when that note says free, no cost, complimentary or $0. Green means the note says nothing else. Amber means the note adds a condition, such as paid parking, a time limit or customers only. Stations with no pricing note are hidden unless you turn on "Show unknown pricing". Always read the sign at the charger before plugging in. Operators change pricing without telling the database.

Rivian plug guide

J1772Level 2. Fits every Rivian directly.
CCSDC fast. Native port on R1T and R1S.
Tesla / NACSNeeds a NACS adapter on a CCS-port Rivian. Native on vehicles with a NACS port. Off by default.
OutletsNEMA 14-50 and similar wall outlets. Only useful if you carry the portable charger. Off by default.

Not a guarantee

Listings can be stale, occupied, broken or behind a gate. The status bar shows when the data was fetched. If a refresh fails, the previous results stay on screen but are marked as stale.
